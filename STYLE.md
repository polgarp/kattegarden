# Kattegården style bible

Locked decisions. Change deliberately, and update this file in the same commit.

## Medium
Everything in the world is a **die-cut sticker on felt**. Each sticker has a 4.5u white border (`#fffdf7`), a hairline die-cut edge (`#c9bfac`), a tight contact shadow and a soft ambient shadow. A sticker that is lifted (being dragged) drops its shadow far below it.

## Terrain vs stickers
- Things she can move are **stickers**: white border and a shadow, and they lift when dragged.
- Things the world owns (tree, puddle, flowerbed, rock) are **felt appliqué** sewn into the yard: a stitched paper-coloured dashed edge, a tiny drop shadow, and no white border. No border means you can't pick it up.
- **Mown checkerboard** grass marks where she can build: one square is one cell. **Long, rough tufts** mark where she can't. There are no grid lines.
- **Sunlight** is a soft beam through a gap in the fence, landing as a warm patch with a dotted edge. It moves with the time of day.

## Shape grammar
- Build only from circles and ellipses, rounded rectangles and soft triangles (ears). Tails are the one freeform curve.
- Rounded rectangle corner radius is about ⅓ of the short side.
- No ink outlines. Shapes separate by colour and shading, never by stroke.

## Size
- **1 cell = one kitten = one cushion**, 36 × 25 units in the yard (34 × 22 drawn, plus gap).
- Small items are 1 cell. Furniture is 2–4 cells wide, or sized by the maths (a bed for 12 is 12 cells).
- Cats: kitten 1 cell, ordinary cat about 3 cells wide, noodle cat 5–6.

## Album
A missing sticker shows as its own silhouette (plum at 15%, border included). The silhouette is the hint, so every cat needs a shape that gives away its cause.

## Light
Always from the top left. Each shape gets its own shading: a shadow crescent at the bottom right (plum `#3b2340` at 24%) and a light rim at the top left (white at 40%). That is the `cel` filter.

## Lines
Only for features: eyes, brows, mouths, stripes, whiskers. Plum `#3b2a4a`, 1.9u at scale 1, round caps. On dark fur, features are yellow `#f6d26b`.

## Faces
Eyes are dots, arcs or half-lidded. Expression comes from lids and brows. Pink blush circles. Small triangle nose.

## Type
Numbers in Baloo 2 Bold. Copy is Danish and minimal: faces, numbers and icons carry the meaning.

## Tone
Cute design, chaotic behaviour. Mistakes are funny, never red. Visual jokes beat words.
