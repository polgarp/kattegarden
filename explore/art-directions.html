Kattegården · phase 1, round 2

Stickers it is

Two passes on the sticker direction. First, shading to give the shapes sharper edges. Second, treating the stickers as real stickers: they peel, they lift, and the rare ones shine.

Shading

Each shape gets a shadow crescent at the bottom right and a light rim at the top left, so the head separates from the body. Option 3 also tightens the sticker itself: a hairline die-cut edge and a crisp contact shadow instead of a blur.

In the yard

As real stickers

A sketch of the physical version: her inventory is a sticker sheet.

  • Placing is sticking. Dragging a piece lifts it off the sheet, the shadow drops away, and letting go slaps it down with a tiny squash.
  • Rare cats are holo stickers. Same cat, shinier, a surprise she can't force. A Pokémon-card feeling without the shop.
  • The merchant sells sheets. Trading becomes "what's on today's sheet," which is a nice reason to reopen the app.
  • The album is the collection. Every cat that visits leaves a sticker of itself, and that page later becomes the picture maker.

What to judge

  1. 1, 2 or 3? Does the shading read as sharper, or just as busier?
  2. Does the sheet make the stickers feel physical enough, or should they go further (curl, gloss, slight paper texture)?
  3. Holo for rare cats: does the shimmer read at phone size?
